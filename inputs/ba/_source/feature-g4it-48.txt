Description

As an inventory manager,
I want to specify whether end-user devices are used in a BYOD or COPE context and optionally override the default professional usage fraction,
so that environmental impacts are allocated according to the RCP SI methodology while retaining flexibility for organization-specific assumptions.
 
Description

The RCP SI methodology specifies that certain end-user devices may be shared between professional and personal contexts. In such cases, only the fraction of impacts attributable to professional use shall be considered in the assessment.

Three usage contexts are defined:

Usage Context
	

Description
	

Professional Usage Fraction

Professional
	

Equipment used exclusively for professional purposes
	

100%

COPE (Corporate Owned, Personally Enabled)
	

Professional equipment that may also be used for personal purposes
	

85%

BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)
	

Personal equipment also used for professional purposes
	

57%

These usage fractions shall be applied to all lifecycle stages of the equipment, including manufacturing, distribution, use, and end-of-life impacts.

To support this methodology, G4IT shall introduce two new optional fields in the Physical Equipment import model:

    contexteUsage

    fractionUsage

The usageContext field allows users to apply the default fractions defined by the RCP SI methodology.

The usageFraction field allows users to explicitly specify a professional usage fraction between 0 and 1. When provided, this value shall take precedence over the fraction associated with the selected usage context.

This functionality shall only apply to end-user devices (e.g. laptops, monitors, smartphones, tablets, printers, internet boxes, etc.) and shall not apply to infrastructure equipment, cloud services, or AI services.

When neither field is provided, the equipment shall be considered a standard professional device and 100% of its impacts shall be allocated to the professional scope.

This approach ensures compliance with the RCP SI methodology while maintaining full backward compatibility with existing inventories and import files.

To improve transparency, dedicated indicators shall also be introduced to provide visibility into BYOD and COPE adoption within inventories.

 
Acceptance criterias
AC1

Given a user imports a physical equipment file

When the file contains the contexteUsage and/or fractionUsage columns

Then both columns shall be considered optional.

And accepted values for contexteUsage shall be:

    BYOD

    COPE

    empty value.

And accepted values for fractionUsage shall be numeric values between 0 and 1 inclusive.

And any other value shall generate a validation error in the rejected file report.

Error label for usageContext

Invalid value for column "contexteUsage". Allowed values are: BYOD, COPE, or empty.

Error label for usageFraction

Invalid value for column "fractionUsage". The value must be a number between 0 and 1.
AC2

Given a physical equipment record has an empty contexteUsage field and an empty fractionUsage field

When environmental impacts are calculated

Then the equipment shall be considered a standard professional device.

And a professional usage fraction of 100% shall be applied.
AC3

Given a physical equipment record has a contexteUsage value of BYOD

And the fractionUsage field is empty

When environmental impacts are calculated

Then 57% of the equipment impacts shall be allocated to the professional scope.

And the 57% allocation shall be applied to all lifecycle stages during calculation.
AC4

Given a physical equipment record has a contexteUsage value of COPE

And the fractionUsage field is empty

When environmental impacts are calculated

Then 85% of the equipment impacts shall be allocated to the professional scope.

And the 85% allocation shall be applied to all lifecycle stages during calculation.
AC5

Given a physical equipment record contains a value in the fractionUsage field

When environmental impacts are calculated

Then the provided fractionUsage value shall take precedence over the default fraction associated with contexteUsage.

And the specified fraction shall be applied to all lifecycle stages during calculation.
AC6

Given a physical equipment record belongs to a category other than Devices

When a contexteUsage or fractionUsage value is provided

Then the values shall be ignored or rejected according to the validation rules defined for the import process.

And the BYOD/COPE methodology shall only apply to end-user devices.
AC7

Given a physical equipment record has been processed by the calculation engine

When the export file is generated for the physical equipment

Then the new columns shall be exported

And the fractionUsage column shall contain the effective fraction used during the calculation.

And default BYOD and COPE fractions shall be exported even when the value was not explicitly provided in the input file.
AC8

Given a user configures Key Indicators (Configure the view panel in the Equipment view)

When the list of available indicators is displayed

Then the following indicators shall be available:

 

Label EN
	

Label FR

BYOD Devices (%)
	

Appareils BYOD (%)

COPE Devices (%)
	

Appareils COPE (%)

And each indicator shall reflect the share of devices (related to the quantity of equipments in the category Devices only) associated with the corresponding usage context.
AC9

Given an existing inventory or import file does not contain the contexteUsage and fractionUsage columns

When impacts are calculated

Then the calculation shall continue to operate without modification.

And all equipment shall be treated as professional equipment with a 100% usage fraction.
